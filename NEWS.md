# metamixr 0.1.0

Initial release.

* `re_mix()`, `sel_mix()` and `sel_flexpb()` fit Bayesian meta-analytic
  mixture models without publication bias adjustment, with a common
  step-function selection model, and with group-specific selection models.
* `sim_mix()` simulates data from the models.
* `mertens_nudge` provides the nudging meta-analysis of Mertens et al. (2022).
* Four vignettes: getting started, an application to the nudging literature,
  resolving multimodality, and a comparison with RoBMA.
